Head Turner – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · App: Head Turner (Android) · Package: com.headturnergame.head_turner

In short: the camera is used only on your phone to read which way your head turns and when you blink. Camera images are never saved, recorded or uploaded. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.

1. Camera

Head Turner asks for camera access so you can steer with your head. Each camera frame is processed on your device by Google ML Kit face detection to measure head turn, head tilt and whether your eyes are open. The frame is then discarded. We do not store, record, transmit or share camera images or face data, and we do not use them to identify you. You can play with touch or tilt controls without granting camera access.

2. Information stored on your phone

Your game progress (distance records, energy cells, cars, missions, streak, settings and calibration) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. You can erase it at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy → Delete my game data.

3. Advertising

Head Turner is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, app usage and ad interactions, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy choices for ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

4. Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase is active.

5. Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates a score-card image and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

6. Children

Head Turner is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security and retention

We do not run servers that store your data. Information held by Google AdMob and Google Play is kept according to Google's policies.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
